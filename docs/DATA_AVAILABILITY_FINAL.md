# Data and code availability

The CICIoT2023 and CICIoMT2024 datasets are available from the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New Brunswick, and IoT-23 from the Stratosphere Laboratory, CTU Prague, under their providers' terms. Experiment scripts, protocols, acquisition manifests, aggregate metrics, and scalar event summaries are available at https://github.com/haryoko-beep/jjkk-label-efficient-calibration. Version 1.0.0 is archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23880279. Raw datasets and fitted caches are excluded; query-level records are regenerated by running the experiments. Supplementary Material S1 documents the evaluation scope and detailed results.
